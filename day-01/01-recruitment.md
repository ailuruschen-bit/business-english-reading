# Unit 01 · 招聘与录用

> **本单元针对你的真实遗忘点**：`applicant` / `apply & application` / `opening` / `eligible for` / `full benefits` / `diploma` / `demeanor` / `punctual` / `stellar` / `possess a keen eye for detail` / `outgoing` / `have a good command of` / `extend employment to` / `subsequently` / `resignation` / `work to deadlines`
>
> **建议用时**：10 分钟（文章 3 分钟，讲解 5 分钟，自测 2 分钟）

---

## Passage A — Job Posting

**Northbridge Media Group — Production Assistant (Full-Time)**

Northbridge Media Group currently has two **openings** for Production Assistants in our Pre-Press Department. Both positions became available following the **resignation** of two long-serving staff members, and we are seeking replacements who can begin work on or before 15 October.

The successful **applicant** will report directly to the Production Manager and will work **extensively** with the design and editorial teams. Duties include preparing files for press, verifying colour accuracy, and maintaining the department's archive of finished artwork.

**Requirements:**

- A diploma or degree in graphic design, print production, or a related field
- At least two years' experience in a **press ad design** environment
- A good command of at least one industry-standard layout application
- A keen eye for detail and the ability to work to tight deadlines
- An outgoing **demeanor** and the confidence to deal with clients directly

Employees are **eligible for** full benefits, including comprehensive medical insurance, after a probationary period of six months. Salary will be commensurate with experience.

Candidates should apply in writing, enclosing a résumé and three samples of recent work. Applications received after 30 September will not be considered. Only those selected for an interview will be contacted; we regret that we cannot respond to every **application** individually.

## Passage B — Cover Letter

Dear Ms. Alvarado,

I am writing to apply for the position of Production Assistant advertised in the 12 September edition of the *Regional Business Weekly*.

I hold a diploma in Print Media from Barton College and have spent the past three years at Kestrel Print, where I was **subsequently** promoted from junior operator to shift supervisor. In that role I was responsible for scheduling, for quality control, and for ensuring that every job left the plant **on schedule**.

Colleagues would describe me as **punctual**, methodical, and comfortable working under pressure. I possess a keen eye for detail — a quality that has served me well in a department where a single misaligned colour plate can cost a client thousands.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience might benefit Northbridge Media Group, and I am available for an interview at your convenience.

Yours sincerely,
Daniel Whitfield

---

## 讲解

### 1. `opening` —— 不是"开口"，是"职位空缺"

托业里 `opening` 最高频的义项是 **a job opening = 一个职位空缺**，可数。

> The hospital has two job **openings** for certified X-ray technicians.

同义替换梯队（Part 7 改写题经常在这几个词之间换）：

| 说法 | 语域 |
|---|---|
| `opening` / `job opening` | 最常见、中性 |
| `vacancy` | 偏英式、正式 |
| `position` | 中性，指"岗位"本身 |
| `post` | 英式正式 |

⚠️ 注意 `vacant` 的另一用法：`shortage of **vacant** office space`＝空置办公空间不足，这里是"空着的"，不是职位。

### 2. `apply` / `application` / `applicant` —— 一个词根三个考点

- **`apply for + 职位/岗位`**：申请。`apply **to** + 机构/人`：向……申请。
  - `apply **for** the position` ✅ ｜ `apply **to** the university` ✅
  - 高频错：<s>apply the position</s>（漏介词）
- **`application`**：① 申请（书）② 应用程序（`layout application`＝排版软件）。两个义项在 Part 7 里都出现。
- **`applicant`**：申请人。注意后缀 `-ant` 表人，对应 Anki 里同族的 `consultant` / `counselor`。

另一组极高频、你卡片里也有的：`apply` 还可以表示"适用"——`This offer does not **apply to** existing members.`

### 3. `be eligible for` —— 有资格享受

> Employees may be **eligible for** additional benefits after one year.

结构固定：`be eligible for + 名词` / `be eligible to do sth`。

辨析（托业最爱的一组）：

| 词 | 含义 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| `eligible` | **有资格**（符合硬性条件） | eligible for a pension |
| `qualified` | **够格**（有能力、有资质） | a qualified accountant |
| `entitled` | **有权利**（法律/合同赋予） | entitled to two weeks' leave |

### 4. `full benefits` / `comprehensive medical insurance`

`benefits` 在职场语境里是**福利**（复数），不是"好处"。
- `full benefits`＝全额福利
- `benefits package`＝福利待遇包
- `comprehensive medical insurance`＝综合医疗保险，`comprehensive` 这里是"覆盖面全的"

⚠️ 别和 `benefit from`（受益于）混。同一篇文章里两个意思都可能出现。

### 5. `demeanor` —— 举止、仪态

> An **outgoing demeanor** and the confidence to deal with clients directly.

`demeanor`（英式 `demeanour`）＝一个人外在表现出来的举止、神态，不可数。招聘广告里几乎固定搭配形容词：

- `a professional / calm / pleasant / outgoing demeanor`

搭档词汇（你卡片里的一串"招聘形容词"）：

| 词 | 意思 | 记忆钩子 |
|---|---|---|
| `outgoing` | 外向的 | 也可指"即将离任的"：the outgoing manager |
| `punctual` | 守时的 | punct-（点）→ 卡在点上 |
| `stellar` | 出色的 | stella＝星 → 明星级的 |
| `courteous` | 有礼貌的（正式） | 比 friendly 正式 |
| `diligent` | 勤勉的 | with diligence and cooperation |

⚠️ `outgoing` 的两个义项都是托业考点，看上下文：`outgoing personality`（外向）vs `outgoing chairman`（即将卸任的）。

### 6. `a keen eye for detail` / `a good command of`

两个在招聘广告里几乎必然出现的固定搭配，**整块背，不要拆**：

- `possess / have a keen eye for detail`＝眼光敏锐、注重细节
- `have a good command of + 语言/技能`＝熟练掌握
  - `a good command of English` / `a good command of the software`
- `work to deadlines`＝按期限工作（注意是 `to`，不是 <s>work on deadlines</s>）
- `work under pressure`＝抗压

### 7. `subsequently` —— 随后

> I was **subsequently** promoted from junior operator to shift supervisor.

`subsequently`＝后来、随后（＝later / afterwards，但更正式）。Part 5 常考副词选择：

| 副词 | 逻辑 |
|---|---|
| `subsequently` | 时间上随后 |
| `consequently` | 因果上因此 |
| `presumably` | 推测：想必 |
| `indifferently` | 冷淡地、不关心地 |

⚠️ 你卡片里 `indifferently vs presumably` 这组——它们长得像但毫无关系：`indifferent`＝漠不关心的（in-＋different，"没差别"→无所谓）；`presumably`＝据推测。

### 8. `extend employment to sb` —— 正式发录用通知

> The company decided to **extend employment to** three of the five finalists.

`extend` 在正式商务英语里＝"给予、提供"，是托业高频的"雅词化"动词：

- `extend employment to sb`＝聘用某人
- `extend an invitation to sb`＝向某人发出邀请
- `extend my sincere condolences to`＝致以诚挚慰问
- `extend business hours`＝延长营业时间 ← 这个才是"延长"的本义

**一个词根，两条线**：空间/时间上的"延长" + 抽象的"给予"。看到 `extend` 先判断后面接的是"时间/范围"还是"人+好意"。

## 自测

**1.** 把下面这句翻译的空填上：医院有两个持证 X 光技师的职位空缺。
The hospital has two job ______ for certified X-ray technicians.

<details><summary>答案</summary>

`openings`。复数。不要写 vacancies 也对，但 TOEIC 原文用 openings 更常见。

</details>

**2.** 选词：Employees are eligible ______ full benefits after six months.
(A) to  (B) for  (C) with  (D) of

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**(B) for**。`be eligible for + 名词`。若后面接动词原形则用 `to`：eligible **to** receive full benefits。

</details>

**3.** 下面哪一句用错了？
(A) She has a good command of Japanese.
(B) He possesses a keen eye for detail.
(C) We need someone who can work on deadlines.
(D) The outgoing chairman gave a farewell speech.

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**(C)** 错。固定搭配是 `work **to** deadlines`（按期限工作）或 `meet deadlines`（赶上期限）。

(D) 没错——`outgoing` 在这里是"即将卸任的"，这是它第二个高频义项。

</details>

**4.** Passage A 中说 "Only those selected for an interview will be contacted"，据此判断：一个在 9 月 28 日投递、但未被选中的申请人，会收到什么？

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**什么都收不到。** 这是 Part 7 推断题的典型设计：广告明说只联系入选者，且"我们无法对每份申请逐一回复"（we cannot respond to every application individually）。

注意日期陷阱：9 月 28 日是**在** 9 月 30 日截止日之前，所以申请有效，只是没入选。如果问的是 10 月 2 日投递，答案就变成"不予考虑"（will not be considered）。托业极爱在日期上设这种两层判断。

</details>
