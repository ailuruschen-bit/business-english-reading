# Unit 02 · 会议、展会与庆典

## Passage A — Invitation

**THE MERIDIAN ARTS COUNCIL**
*requests the pleasure of your company at our*
**Inaugural Gala Celebration**

Saturday, 18 October · 7:00 p.m.
The Ashworth Rooms, 44 Calder Street

Please join us as we mark twenty-five years of service to the region. The evening will **commence** promptly at seven with a reception in the west gallery, after which the Council's Chair will **propose a toast to** our **distinguished guests**. Dinner will be served at eight, and the curtains go up on our anniversary programme at nine.

A **special feature** of the evening will be a short recital by three **luminaries** of the regional music scene, two of whom began their careers on Council scholarships.

All **proceeds from this event** will be contributed to the continued operation of the Council's community studios.

Tickets: $120 per person, or $1,100 for a table of ten.
**RSVP by 4 October** to events@meridianarts.org.

*Formal dress. Complete dinners are included; the cash bar is excluded from the ticket price.*

## Passage B — Trade Show Notice to Exhibitors

**BAY CITY EXPOSITION CENTRE — Notice to Exhibitors, Autumn Auto Show**

Exhibitors are reminded that move-in begins at 6:00 a.m. on Thursday. The Centre will supply **partitions** and **lecterns** to all booths in Halls B and C at no additional charge; **pop-up displays** exceeding 2.4 metres in height must be approved by the floor manager in advance.

Our company has been organizing **conventions** for the last fifteen years, and we ask exhibitors to observe the following:

1. All packing material must **be disposed of properly** in the designated bays. Exhibitors who leave crates in the aisles **may be subject to** a clean-up charge.
2. Electrical equipment found to be **malfunctioning** will be disconnected by Centre staff without notice.
3. All equipment supplied by the Centre must **remain Centre property** after the event.
4. Exhibitors must give **24 hours' notice** of any intention to alter their stand layout.

Please note that the Small Business **Seminar** originally scheduled for Friday morning will now take place on Friday afternoon, **as was first planned**. **Due to popular demand**, a second **introductory session** has been added at 4:00 p.m.

## 讲解

### 1. 活动四兄弟：`conference` / `convention` / `seminar` / `exposition`

这是托业阅读的经典考点，也是你 Anki 里明确标出的困惑。核心差别在**规模**和**目的**：

| 词 | 规模 | 目的 | 典型搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `seminar` | 最小，几十人 | **学习/培训**，有互动 | attend a seminar on marketing |
| `lecture` | 小，单向 | **一个人讲，听众听** | give a lecture |
| `conference` | 中到大 | **同行交流**，多场分会 | an academic conference |
| `convention` | 大 | **行业/组织的年度大聚会** | the annual sales convention |
| `exposition` (= `expo`) | 大 | **展示产品**，有展位 | the auto show / trade exposition |

记忆钩子：
- `sem-` 想成 seed（种子）→ 小规模播种式教学
- `con-vene`＝共同（con）来（vene）→ 全体成员来一趟 → 大会
- `ex-pos-ition`＝向外（ex）摆出来（pos）→ 展览

⚠️ 你卡片里 `lecture vs. conference / as part of` 这一条，考的其实是 **`as part of`**：
> The lecture is offered **as part of** the three-day conference.
> 这场讲座是三天会议的**一个组成部分**。

所以两者不是并列关系，是包含关系。Part 7 里常这样问："The lecture is ___ ?" 答案 "one event within a larger conference"。

还有一个你卡片里的说法：`large, organized affairs`。`affair` 在这里＝**（有组织的）活动、场合**，不是"事务/绯闻"。
> Conventions are large, organized **affairs** that require months of planning.

### 2. 展会硬件词：`partition` / `lectern` / `pop-up display` / `booth`

这些词只在展会场景出现，但一出现就是细节题的答案：

- `partition` [pɑːrˈtɪʃn]＝**隔板、隔断**（把大厅分成展位的板子）。动词义"分割"。
- `lectern` [ˈlektərn]＝**讲台、发言台**（放讲稿的斜面小台子）。注意不是 `podium`（严格说 podium 是站的台子，但美式英语常混用）。
- `pop-up display`＝**便携式易拉宝/折叠展架**（一拉就弹起来）。
- `booth`＝展位、摊位。`stand` 是英式同义词（Passage B 用了 `stand layout`）。

### 3. 庆典套话：整块记忆

托业的邀请函高度程式化，下面这些**整句背下来**，考试时一眼扫过即可：

| 套话 | 意思 |
|---|---|
| `requests the pleasure of your company at` | 敬邀您出席（最正式的邀请开头） |
| `commence promptly at seven` | 七点整开始 |
| `propose a toast to sb` | 向某人致祝酒辞 |
| `raise your glass to sb` | 举杯致敬（比 toast 口语） |
| `the curtains go up on` | 大幕拉开 → 正式开演 |
| `a special feature of the evening` | 当晚的特别节目 |
| `in celebration of its 25th anniversary` | 庆祝 25 周年 |
| `all proceeds will be contributed to` | 全部收益将捐给 |
| `RSVP by 4 October` | 请于 10 月 4 日前回复 |

**`RSVP`** 来自法语 *Répondez s'il vous plaît*＝请回复。既可作动词（`Please RSVP to…`）也可作名词。

**`luminary`**＝名流、杰出人物（lumin-＝光 → 发光的人）。复数 `luminaries`。近义：`dignitary`（政要）、`distinguished guests`（贵宾）。

**`inaugural`** [ɪˈnɔːɡjərəl]＝**首届的、就职的**。
- `the inaugural gala`＝首届晚宴
- `an inaugural speech`＝就职演说
- 动词 `inaugurate`＝为……举行开幕/就职典礼

### 4. `proceeds` / `feature` / `commence` —— 三个"伪装成熟词"的生词

- **`proceeds`**（永远复数，重音在前 ˈproʊsiːdz）＝**收益、所得款项**。
  ⚠️ 和动词 `proceed`（prəˈsiːd，继续进行）**不是一个词**，只是同形。
  > All **proceeds** from this event will be donated. ✅ 名词
  > Please **proceed** to Gate 12. ✅ 动词

- **`feature`**：
  - 名词＝特色、特写文章（`a special feature`）
  - 动词＝以……为特色、由……主演
  - **被动高频**：`be featured in`＝被刊登在/被收录进
  > The winning pieces will be **featured in** our Art Book.

- **`commence`**＝开始（比 begin/start 正式得多，托业书面通知专用）。
  名词 `commencement`＝① 开始 ② **毕业典礼**（美式，这个义项也考）。

### 5. `be subject to` —— 一个必须双向掌握的短语

> Exhibitors who leave crates in the aisles **may be subject to** a clean-up charge.

`be subject to + 名词`＝**可能受到、须服从于**（常带"不利后果"或"取决于"的味道）：

- `subject to change`＝可能变动（价目表下的小字）
- `subject to approval`＝须经批准
- `be subject to a fine`＝可能被罚款

⚠️ 别和 `be subjected to`（遭受，被施加）混，后者更强烈：`be subjected to criticism`。

同族高频：`be disposed of properly`（被妥善处理掉）。注意 **`dispose of` 是不可分短语动词**，被动时 `of` 必须保留：
> All packing material must be **disposed of** properly. ✅
> <s>must be disposed properly</s> ❌ ← 这正是你卡片里 "of in dispose of" 标记的点。

### 6. `as was first planned` —— 一个小而恶心的语法点

> The seminar will now take place on Friday afternoon, **as was first planned**.

这里的 `as` 是**关系代词**，指代前面整个句子的内容，在从句里作主语。所以不能加 `it`：

| 写法 | 判断 |
|---|---|
| `as was planned` | ✅ as 本身就是主语 |
| `as planned` | ✅ 省略式，最常用 |
| `as it was planned` | ❌ 主语重复 |
| `as was first planned` | ✅ 加副词无妨 |

同类结构：`as was expected` / `as is often the case` / `as we agreed`。

**逻辑陷阱**：`as was first planned` 意思是"**恢复**到最初的安排"，说明中间改过一次。Part 7 常据此出题："What does the notice suggest about the seminar?" → 答案是 "Its schedule had been changed previously."

### 7. `due to popular demand` / `introductory session`

- `due to popular demand`＝**应广大要求**（固定套话，常用来解释"加场/延期/返场"）
- `introductory session`＝**入门场次/介绍性课程**。`introductory` 还有"促销性的"义项：`an introductory offer`＝新客优惠价。

## 自测

**1.** 选词：The three-day ______ will include twelve seminars, a trade exhibition, and a closing banquet.
(A) lecture  (B) convention  (C) appointment  (D) interview

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**(B) convention**。三天、包含十二场研讨会 + 展览 + 晚宴 → 规模最大、包罗最广的那个词。`lecture` 只是单场讲座，装不下十二个 seminar。

</details>

**2.** 改错：All packing material must be disposed properly.

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

→ `must be disposed **of** properly`。

`dispose of` 是不可分短语动词，被动语态里介词 `of` 不能丢。类似的还有：`take care of` → `be taken care of`；`deal with` → `be dealt with`。

</details>

**3.** Passage A 中，票价 $120 **不包含**下列哪一项？
(A) 招待会  (B) 正餐  (C) 酒水  (D) 周年演出

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**(C) 酒水**。最后一行小字：`the cash bar is excluded from the ticket price`。

`cash bar`＝**自费酒水吧**（相对的 `open bar`＝免费畅饮）。这个词本身就是答案的一半——看到 "cash" 就该意识到要自己掏钱。

顺带：`Complete dinners are included` 呼应你卡片里的 `complete dinners are excluded`——托业原题里这个短语是**排除**的，我这里故意反过来写，就是要你别靠记忆答题、靠读文章答题。

</details>

**4.** "All proceeds from this event will be contributed to the continued operation of the Council's community studios." 这句话说明这场晚宴的性质是什么？

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**是一场募捐/慈善活动（a fundraiser）**，不是单纯的庆祝。

`proceeds`（收益）+ `be contributed to`（捐给）= 筹款。你卡片里有 `Fulton County Fundraiser` 这一条，正是同一个场景。Part 7 主旨题常这样问："What is the main purpose of the event?" 别被 "Gala Celebration" 的标题带偏——**标题说庆祝，正文说捐款，答案跟正文走。**

</details>
