## Passage A — Transit Notice

**METRO TRANSIT AUTHORITY — Service Advisory**

**Effective Monday, 6 October: Weekday Delays on the Green Line**

Because of **ongoing construction** at Calder Junction, Green Line trains will operate at reduced frequency between 9:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. on weekdays. The pedestrian bridge at the north entrance is also **undergoing construction** and will remain closed until spring.

**Commuters** travelling during peak hours should not be affected. Passengers who normally travel mid-morning should allow an additional fifteen minutes.

The Authority regrets **the inconvenience that these delays are causing passengers** and thanks riders for their patience. Shuttle buses will **take the place of** trains on the Calder–Westfield segment; the replacement service will **take place** from the surface stop on Weir Street, not from the platform.

Should there be any further changes to the schedule, we will notify riders **promptly** through the Authority's website and station displays. Station staff are **only a phone call away** at 1-800-555-0144 and will be happy to assist.

*Please note: bicycles may not be carried on shuttle buses. Riders are asked to **dispose of** all litter **properly** before boarding.*

## Passage B — Customer Service Email

Dear Ms. Bergström,

Thank you for your message of 2 October regarding your stay at the Harbourview.

I have reviewed your file and can confirm that our reservations team attempted to reach you by telephone **numerous times** on the afternoon of 28 September. Our standard **procedure** is to place a **confirmation call** twenty-four hours before arrival; when a guest cannot be reached, the room is held until 6:00 p.m. only. **It just so happens that** a conference block had filled the hotel that week, and the room was released at six.

I understand this explanation does not undo your inconvenience, and **in this respect** I would like to offer a complimentary night's stay, valid for twelve months.

I should add that our front-desk team has been **courteous** and professional throughout this matter, and I have asked them to **cater to** both your dietary requirements and your preference for a quiet floor should you choose to return.

Our restaurant has recently **extended its business hours** to 11:00 p.m., and the fitness centre now opens at 5:30 a.m. If you are ever in the area, please feel free to **drop by** — no reservation is necessary.

Kind regards,
Priya Raghunathan, Guest Relations Manager

## 讲解

### 1. `ongoing` vs. `undergoing` —— 两个长得像但语法角色完全不同的词

这是你 Anki 里明确标出的一对，也是 Part 5 的常客：

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 句法位置 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ongoing` | **形容词** | 正在进行中的、持续的 | 放在名词**前面**：ongoing construction |
| `undergo` | **动词** | 经历、接受（改造/手术/检查） | 主语 **is undergoing** 某事 |

对照两句：

> Delays are caused by **ongoing construction**. ✅ 形容词 + 名词
> The bridge **is undergoing construction**. ✅ 主语正在"经历"施工

⚠️ 错法：<s>The bridge is ongoing construction.</s> ❌ —— 形容词不能当谓语用在这里。

`undergo` 的三态：**undergo – underwent – undergone**（不规则）。
常见搭配：`undergo renovation / repairs / surgery / a transformation / testing`

另外，`ongoing` 现代拼写是**一个词**，不写 <s>on-going</s>。

### 2. `take place` vs. `take the place of` —— 一个 the 改变一切

这组你卡片里也点了名：

| 短语 | 意思 | 主语是谁 |
|---|---|---|
| `take place` | **举行、发生** | **事件**（会议、演出、延误） |
| `take the place of` | **取代、替代** | **人或物** |
| `replace` | 取代（及物动词） | 人或物 |

> The ceremony will **take place** on Friday. ＝典礼周五举行
> Shuttle buses will **take the place of** trains. ＝班车将取代列车
> ＝ Shuttle buses will **replace** trains.（同义改写）

⚠️ `take place` **没有被动语态**——它本身就表示"发生"：
<s>The meeting was taken place.</s> ❌

Passage A 故意把两个短语放进同一段，就是让你在真实语境里分辨。

### 3. `commuter` / `rider` / `passenger` —— 谁是谁

- **`commuter`**＝**通勤者**（每天往返上下班的人）。动词 `commute`＝通勤；名词也可指"通勤路程"（`a two-hour commute`）
- **`rider`**＝乘客（公共交通语境的美式说法）
- **`passenger`**＝乘客（通用，飞机/火车/汽车都行）
- **`peak hours` / `rush hour`**＝高峰时段 ｜ `off-peak`＝非高峰

Part 7 细节题陷阱：通知说 "Commuters travelling during **peak hours** should not be affected"，问 "Who is most likely to be affected?" → 答案是**非高峰时段（mid-morning）出行的人**，因为限速时段是 9:00–3:00，正好避开早晚高峰。**读时刻表类通知，把时间段和受影响人群配对画出来。**

### 4. `the inconvenience that the delay is causing passengers` —— 一个双宾语结构

这句在托业道歉语里高频出现，值得拆开看：

```
the inconvenience [that the delay is causing passengers]
                   └─ 定语从句，that 是 causing 的直接宾语
                      cause + 人 + 物 = 给某人造成某物
```

`cause sb sth`＝给某人造成某事（双宾语），也可写成 `cause sth to sb`。

标准道歉套话（整块背）：
- `We apologize for any inconvenience caused.`
- `We regret the inconvenience this may cause.`
- `Thank you for your patience during this period.`
- `considerable inconvenience`＝相当大的不便（你卡片里有）

⚠️ `inconvenience` 通常**不可数**：`any inconvenience` ✅ / <s>an inconvenience</s>（口语里有，但托业书面语按不可数用）。

### 5. `prompt` —— 形容词、副词、动词全是考点

这个词你卡片里出现了两次（`prompt` 和 `promptly`），说明是真痛点：

| 词形 | 词性 | 意思 |
|---|---|---|
| `prompt` | 形容词 | **及时的、迅速的** — a prompt reply |
| `prompt` | 形容词 | **准时的** — Please be prompt during meal times. |
| `prompt` | 动词 | **促使、引发** — The complaint prompted a review. |
| `promptly` | 副词 | ① 立刻 ② **整（点）** — commence promptly at seven |

⚠️ 第四行的两个义项要靠上下文分：
- `Please notify us **promptly**.`＝请**立即**通知我们
- `The gala will commence **promptly** at seven.`＝七点**整**开始

近义辨析：
- `prompt`＝快（反应迅速）｜ `punctual`＝准时（不迟到）
- `promptly` ≈ `immediately` ≈ `without delay` ≈ `at once`

### 6. `go off` / `on schedule` / `drop by` / `be excused` —— 四个小短语

- **`go off`**（警报/闹钟）**响起**；也可指"爆炸"、"（食物）变质"（英式）
  > The alarm system will **go off** if someone tries to enter after hours.
  ⚠️ 这个义项反直觉：中文"响"对应英文"go off"（原意"启动、触发"）
- **`on schedule`**＝**按计划、准时**
  > The automobile parts arrived **on schedule**.
  对比：`ahead of schedule`（提前）/ `behind schedule`（延误）/ `on time`（准点，更口语）
- **`drop by`** ＝ `drop in` ＝ `stop by`＝**顺道拜访**（不预约、随意）
  > I can **drop by** this afternoon.
- **`be excused`**＝**获准离开/免除**
  > After dinner, may I **be excused**?＝我可以离席了吗？（餐桌礼貌用语）
  > Students may be **excused from** the final exam.＝可免考

### 7. `cater to` / `courteous` / `procedure` / `in this respect`

- **`cater to sb/sth`**＝**迎合、满足（需求）**；`cater **for** an event`＝为活动供餐
  > We cater **to** both business travellers and families.
  ⚠️ `cater to both A and B` 里 **both…and…** 连接的两项必须**词性结构对称**（Passage B 里是 your dietary requirements / your preference —— 两个名词短语）。这是 Part 5 平行结构题的考法。
- **`courteous`** [ˈkɜːrtiəs]＝**有礼貌的、周到的**（比 `polite` 正式，比 `friendly` 拘谨）。名词 `courtesy`
  - `courtesy` 还有"免费赠送"的形容词义：`a courtesy shuttle`＝免费接驳车、`courtesy call`＝礼节性拜访
- **`procedure`**＝**程序、步骤**。`follow a procedure`＝按流程办
  - `standard procedure`＝标准流程 ｜ `safety procedures`＝安全规程
  - ⚠️ `proceed`（继续进行）是动词，`procedure` 是名词，`proceeds` 是"收益"（见 Unit 02）——**三兄弟别混**
- **`in this respect`**＝**在这一点上、就此而言**
  近义：`in this regard` / `on this point`
  ⚠️ `respect` 作"方面"讲是可数名词：`in many respects`＝在许多方面。和"尊敬"是同一个词，靠搭配分

### 8. `It just so happens that…` —— 口语化的"碰巧"

> **It just so happens that** a conference block had filled the hotel that week.

＝**碰巧、恰好**（语气比 `happen to` 更强调巧合，带一点"不巧"的意味）。

同族：
- `It happens that…`＝碰巧
- `S + happen to do`＝碰巧做某事 — `I happened to be in the area.`
- `as it happens`＝碰巧（插入语）

**语境判断**：客服信里用这个短语，往往是在**解释一个对客户不利的巧合**——后面跟的是"所以房间被释放了"。Part 7 推断题会问 "Why was the room not held?" → 因为酒店被会议团队订满 + 联系不上客人。

### 9. `numerous times` / `only a phone call away` / `extend business hours`

- `numerous times`＝**多次**（`numerous`＝众多的，比 many 正式）
- `only a phone call away`＝**一个电话就能找到**（away 表"距离"的比喻用法）
  同款：`help is just a click away`＝点一下就能获得帮助
- `extend business hours`＝**延长营业时间**（对照 Unit 01 的 `extend employment to`——同一个动词的"延长"本义）

## 自测

**1.** 选词：The historic bridge is currently ______ extensive renovation.
(A) ongoing  (B) undergoing  (C) going off  (D) taking place

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**(B) undergoing**。主语 the bridge "正在经历"翻修，需要**动词**。

(A) 是形容词，不能这样作谓语。(D) `take place` 主语必须是事件，而且不及物、不能带宾语 renovation。

</details>

**2.** 改写：Shuttle buses will take the place of trains. 用 replace 改写。

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

→ Shuttle buses will **replace** trains.

⚠️ 注意方向别搞反：A takes the place of B ＝ A replaces B ＝ **A 顶替 B**。
反过来说是 "B is replaced **by** A"。托业改写题最爱在这个方向上设陷阱。

</details>

**3.** 选词：The ceremony will ______ in the main auditorium.
(A) be taken place  (B) take place  (C) take the place  (D) be held place

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**(B) take place**。

`take place` 是不及物短语，**没有被动式**，所以 (A) 错。想用被动就换动词：`will **be held** in the main auditorium` ✅

</details>

**4.** 根据 Passage A，哪一类乘客最可能受到影响？

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**上午 9 点到下午 3 点之间乘车的人**（原文点名 "Passengers who normally travel mid-morning"）。

通勤者（peak hours）明确"不受影响"。这是典型的"排除式细节题"——文章先告诉你谁**不**受影响，答案在剩下的人里。

</details>

**5.** Passage B 里，Raghunathan 女士提供的补偿是什么？有什么限制？

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**补偿**：免费住一晚（a complimentary night's stay）。
**限制**：十二个月内有效（valid for twelve months）。

`complimentary`＝**免费赠送的** ← 高频考点，⚠️ 别和 `complementary`（互补的）混。
- `complimentary breakfast`＝免费早餐
- `complementary colours`＝互补色

两个词只差一个字母（**i** vs **e**），托业 Part 5 会直接把它们放进同一道题的选项里。记忆钩子：compl**i**mentary 里的 **i** 想成 "**I** pay nothing"。

</details>
